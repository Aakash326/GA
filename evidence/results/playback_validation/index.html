Playback benchmark validation

Saved audio and chunk arrival times reconstructed into WAV playback. Initial silence represents measured startup; later inserted silence represents buffer underruns. These are offline reconstructions, not new live or handset recordings. Delayed starts use future arrivals and are not a universal buffer recommendation.

Buffer and tolerance sensitivity

Fixed startup prebuffers of 0, 50, 100 and 200 ms were tested with 1, 10 and 20 ms reporting tolerances (168 stream/condition rows). The tested v3.0 traces underrun in every condition; the two v2.5 controls do not. These controls do not establish general model superiority.
