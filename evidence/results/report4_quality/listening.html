Independent listening study

Work independently as L1, L2 or L3. Model and condition names are hidden. Do not open the separate mapping key before submitting. First listen and write what you hear, then reveal the reference for pronunciation/expression ratings. Existing knowledge of the voices may reduce blinding.

1 = severe problems; 3 = noticeable issues but understandable; 5 = natural/appropriate/clear. Leave a score blank when unable to judge. This small panel is not an industry MOS benchmark.

Listening ratings

Scores are kept only in this browser until you export. Send the exported CSV for import; no network submission occurs. The response sheet in the repository remains blank until actual human scores are supplied.
